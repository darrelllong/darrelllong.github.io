# UCO and Lick: working source notes

Checked October 25, 2026. Post: `posts/2026-10-25-uco-lick.md`.

- **Adaptive optics mirror hardware.** The [1995 instrument paper by Olivier, Max, and colleagues](https://opg.optica.org/abstract.cfm?uri=ADOP-1995-MC4) specifies a continuous-surface deformable mirror with 127 actuators for the early Lick system. [LLNL's explanation](https://space-science.llnl.gov/about/foundations-adaptive-optics) describes hundreds to thousands of actuators making local adjustments of hundreds of nanometers at rates up to a thousand times per second. The post now describes the array of actuators and its many local adjustments instead of saying simply “a small, flexible mirror.” The actuators should not be described as separate little mirrors. The later [ShaneAO instrument design](https://lao.ucolick.org/ShaneAO/tech-docs/ShaneAODocument.html) uses two correcting mirrors, with 52 and 1,024 actuators respectively.

- **General Hyde's title and military service.** Use Brigadier General Harold Hyde, U.S. Army Reserve, retired, on first reference and General Hyde thereafter. His [obituary preserved by UCSC Emeriti](https://emeriti.ucsc.edu/Obituaries/HydeHal.pdf) confirms combat as a second lieutenant in Patton's Third Army in 1945 and retirement from the Army Reserve as a brigadier general in 1983.

- **Colleges today.** The colleges continue to exist. The [2026–2027 catalog](https://catalog.ucsc.edu/en/current/general-catalog/undergraduate-information/undergraduate-academic-program/college-requirements) confirms each college's core course requirement for first-year students. Stevenson also lists a second course, so the draft does not say every college offers exactly one course. Darrell's characterization of the core course as indoctrination is his assessment and is retained directly.

- **Founding dates.** UCSC and UCI opened in 1965. UCSD was established in 1960 and admitted its first undergraduates in 1964. The California Master Plan is from 1960; planning and authorization of the new campuses had already begun in the 1950s. The draft therefore says they emerged during the Master Plan era. Sources: [UCSC](https://news.ucsc.edu/2009/04/a-sustaining-vision/), [UCI planning history](https://planningandsustainability.uci.edu/physical/pdf/campus-lrdp/chapter1.pdf), [UCSD](https://today.ucsd.edu/story/uc-san-diego-turns-60), [first undergraduate class](https://revelle.ucsd.edu/about/history/charter%20class.html), [original Master Plan](https://www.ucop.edu/acadinit/mastplan/MasterPlan1960.pdf).

- **Oxford and Caltech.** Darrell identifies Harold Hyde, his dear friend and UCSC's founding vice chancellor for business and finance under Dean McHenry, as his firsthand source. Preserve this attribution. Hyde's [oral history](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/5rq98388) describes the founding, academic organization, and Lick transfer. The exact Oxford/Caltech comparison was not located in that transcript; the draft attributes it to Darrell's conversations with Hyde. Independent institutional support: UCSC's [college history](https://library.ucsc.edu/reg-hist/collegenineandten) discusses Oxford's influence, higher-than-promised costs, the graduate-weighted allocation formula, and Sinsheimer's reorganization. UCSD's [University House planning history](https://plandesignbuild.ucsd.edu/_files/plans/UniversityHouseLMP.pdf) explicitly identifies Caltech as the standard for the proposed graduate science and engineering campus.

- **Physics impact.** The verified 2007 comparison ranked UCSC first in physics among the top 100 federally funded U.S. universities, using relative citation impact for papers from 2001–2005. The same [campus report](https://news.ucsc.edu/2007/02/ucsc-ranked-first-in-nation-for-research-impact-in-physics/) records a first-place U.S. physics ranking in 2000. These are historical physics-wide measures, not a current ranking specifically of particle physics.

- **UCO's remit.** [UCO's mission](https://www.ucobservatories.org/about/mission/) and [UCSC Astronomy](https://astronomy.ucsc.edu/about/) confirm the headquarters at UCSC, operation of Lick, and administration of UC's Keck participation. Keck is a partnership, operated through CARA; UCO does not operate every observatory in Hawaiʻi. Both Keck telescopes are on Maunakea. UCO also coordinates UC participation in the Thirty Meter Telescope project; the draft does not describe that project as an operating observatory.

- **Lick chronology.** Operations began in 1888. The Regents' action incorporating Lick into UCSC was November 20, 1964. The physical move was November 1966, as Albert Whitford specifies in his [oral history](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/4t8949gc). UCO's mission page uses 1964 for the headquarters move; the draft distinguishes administrative action from relocation. UCO was created in 1988, according to the [2009 Lick strategic plan](https://www.ucolick.org/home/about/governance/2009StrategicPlanningLickObserv.pdf). Hyde's description of Lick as “a wonderful jump start in the physical sciences” is on printed page 93, PDF page 95 of [his transcript](https://escholarship.org/content/qt5rq98388/qt5rq98388_noSplash_5e34a7fb7c46d0a2b2cf7eccc31cc005.pdf#page=95).

- **Adaptive optics versus sodium laser guide stars.** The general adaptive-optics proposal predates the JASON work: Horace Babcock published it in 1953. The draft focuses on Max and colleagues' sodium laser guide star work and Lick's role in demonstrating it. The [1994 paper](https://opg.optica.org/josaa/abstract.cfm?uri=josaa-11-1-263) names William Happer, Gordon MacDonald, Claire Max, and Freeman Dyson. [Foy and Labeyrie's paper](https://pascal-francis.inist.fr/vibad/index.php?action=getRecordDetail&idt=8087661) appeared in 1985. [Bauman and Gavel's Livermore history](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/15005080) dates declassification to 1991, the Livermore experiment to 1993, and sodium-guide-star image improvement at Lick to 1996. The draft does not claim that the French publication immediately caused declassification. Sources differ on 1983 versus 1984 for the initial sodium proposal; “early 1980s” covers the documented sequence.

- **Claire Max's appointments.** [UCSC's 2004 profile](https://news.ucsc.edu/2004/09/ucsc-astronomer-claire-max-receives-2004-e-o-lawrence-award-in-physics/) gives LLNL in 1974, associate director of the newly established CfAO in 1999, and UCSC faculty in 2001. She later directed CfAO. Do not describe her as arriving in 2001 as the center's founding director; the 2004 account still identifies her as deputy director. Her [2015 UCO appointment](https://news.ucsc.edu/2015/08/uco-director/) followed service as interim director beginning in 2014.

- **Richard A. Muller.** Darrell identifies his involvement in JASON adaptive optics and knows him personally. Muller's [2005 CV](https://muller.lbl.gov/pages/MullerVita-03-05.htm) confirms experimental adaptive optics in 1973–1977 and JASON service beginning in 1973, including its steering committee in 1980–1982. The draft states these documented dates directly, without a recollection qualifier. They establish earlier adaptive optics work; the CV does not by itself identify his role in the later sodium-guide-star study. Search results also include Richard S. Muller of Berkeley EECS, a different person involved in MEMS adaptive optics; do not conflate them.

- **Grades.** The [Senate manual](https://senate.ucsc.edu/senate-division-manual/appendices/) retains the October 1996 interpretation “Letter Grades in Narrative Evaluations,” addressing the advisory status of CEP's guidelines. Darrell supplies the firsthand account of his committee service and the ruling's effect. [Fall 1997](https://news.ucsc.edu/?p=60337) brought broad access to requested letter grades and GPAs; the [2000 vote](https://currents.ucsc.edu/99-00/02-21/grades.html) made conventional grading the default for entering students in fall 2001. Narrative evaluations continued alongside grades and became instructor-optional in 2010. Employment consequences at HP and in government remain Darrell's account, not a claim established here about a universal employer policy.

- **Cover and sodium resonance.** Built-in image_gen generated an illustration, saved as `images/lick-laser-guide-star.png`. The exact prompt and provenance are in `docs/lick-laser-guide-star-image.json`. The golden beam represents sodium D₂ excitation near 589 nm in the mesospheric sodium layer around 90 km altitude. [ESO's technical manual](https://archive.eso.org/wdb/help/eso/WLGSU_CANARY_AO_Manual.pdf) gives 589.16 nm in vacuum. [ESO's explanation](https://www.eso.org/public/blog/how-lasers-are-shining-light-on-cosmos/) explains the orange-yellow appearance and fluorescence. The artwork is an illustration of the setting and mechanism, not a documentary photograph or architectural survey.
