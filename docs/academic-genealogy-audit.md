# Academic genealogy audit

Checked September 21, 2026, at the owner's request, following biography
revision `9204f7e01f6abe282ea519158f0d364cb3f68987`.

## Finding and biography change

The [Mathematics Genealogy Project record for Darrell Long](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/id.php?id=10794)
lists **23 doctoral students and 41 academic descendants in total**. The 41
includes the 23 direct students; it does not mean 41 additional descendants.
The recorded second generation comprises sixteen students of Randal Burns,
one of Ahmed Amer, and one of Richard Golding. Thus 23 + 16 + 1 + 1 = 41.
The three branch pages were read directly, and their counts and names agree
with Long's record. No third generation is recorded on those branch pages.

The biography now names the database, dates its count to September 2026,
and explains that Long's students have themselves supervised doctoral
research. The heading is now “Education, teaching, and mentoring.” This adds
substance that the earlier generic description of supervision omitted.
It reports the database's documented lineage, without presenting MGP as a
complete census of every student or later descendant.

The general web reader could not retrieve the MGP record. The actual pages
loaded in the browser, where the names, tables, advisor relationships, degree
information, and total counts were inspected. No database changes were submitted.

## Differences from the CV

The January 2026 CV's completed-doctorate table on PDF page 53 has nineteen
names. MGP adds four names to that table: Bel, Hughes, Barker, and Xie.

- Bel, Hughes, and Barker remain under “Advisor to Continuing Students” on
  PDF page 52. Their individual MGP pages identify Long as advisor and give
  completed degrees in 2020, 2021, and 2022 respectively. The
  [CRSS profile for Bel](https://www.crss.us/person/obel.html) independently
  says she completed her Ph.D.; Barker's
  [2022 HPTS presentation](https://hpts.ws/papers/2022/HPTS-2022-Students.pdf)
  describes his recent graduation and Long's advising. These show that the
  CV's continuing-student list is stale despite the document's 2026 date.
- Xie's individual MGP page lists Dan Feng and Darrell Long as advisors of
  his 2013 doctorate. This is coadvising, not a UCSC degree.
- Committee-service lists were not added to the student count. In particular,
  the CV's reading-committee entry for Sinjoni Mukhopadhyay is not evidence of
  a completed 2019 Ph.D. under Long. Her
  [UCSC account](https://inrg.engineering.ucsc.edu/people/sinjoni-mukhopadhyay-king/)
  gives March 2023 and identifies Katia Obraczka and Faisal Nawab as her final
  advisors. No additional direct student was inferred from that CV entry.

The biography does not repeat the stale continuing-student claim. The CV PDF
itself has not been edited; updating its advising tables remains a separate
source-document correction. The MGP count is explicitly attributed and dated
rather than asserted as a verified lifetime total.
