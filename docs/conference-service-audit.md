Date: September 21, 2026. Starting commit:

The biography previously named WMCSA and FAST but omitted MASCOTS and MSST
leadership and gave no sense of the extent of Long's committee work. The
owner identified this omission. The revision retains the education-first
structure and adds the breadth and duration of conference service to the
professional-service section. The owner then emphasized that the biography
must remain a narrative, not a catalog. Accordingly, exact appointment

larger research community; it omits the list of subject areas and individual

## Counts from the CV

The Conference Organization section of the [January 7, 2026 CV](../cv.pdf),
PDF pages 26–30, lists:

| Role | Listed entries |

| Program committee | 80 |
| Steering committee | 47 |
| Program chair, including founding program chair | 6 |
| General chair, including founding general chair | 7 |

These count venue-and-role entries under the CV's year headings, not distinct
conference series or independently verified lifetime totals. A committee
appointment repeated in different years counts once in each listed year.
Chair entries are kept separate from ordinary committee entries; a program
chair is not silently counted a second time as a program committee member.
Reviewer, advisory, publicity, and local-arrangements roles are excluded.
The combined 1991 local-arrangements/program-committee entry contributes one
program committee entry. The two 1994 blocks and two 1996 blocks are combined
within their respective years.

The CV separately lists DSN and its DCCS symposium in 2006; both are counted
as listed entries, not asserted to be two independent appointments. Its
conference service ends with 2024 entries despite the CV's 2026 date, and it
does not enumerate all of the owner's service. In particular, MASCOTS 2019
program committee service and MSST 2026 roles appear in conference records
but not in these CV counts. No sum is presented as a complete career total.
The biography uses “dozens” rather than turning these documentary counts
into an unqualified public statistic.

## Leadership evidence and wording

| Biography claim | Evidence and limits |
| --- | --- |
| Dozens of program committees | The CV tally above supports the breadth statement. The public biography does not give an exact total. |
| Founding member of the MASCOTS steering committee; served for decades | The owner's firsthand correction supplies founding membership and duration. CV steering entries span 2003–2022; omissions in individual years do not establish breaks in service. No unverified start or end year is assigned. |
| MASCOTS steering committee chair | The [official 2019 organizers page](https://sites.google.com/view/mascots-2019/organizers) explicitly names Darrell Long as chair. It also lists him on the program committee, an appointment absent from the CV's 2019 program-committee entries. |
| MASCOTS general chair and program chair | CV, PDF pages 27–29, records general chair in 1999 and 2014 and program chair in 2010. The biography includes the roles without listing each year. |
| MSST general chair for several years; steering committee member | Owner's firsthand correction; CV general-chair entry for 2024; [official MSST 2026 call for papers](https://www.msstconference.org/2026/research-cfp.html) names him as both general chair and steering committee member. No exact starting year is invented. The 2025 archive contains an agenda but no chair roster, so it is not presented as proof of that year's role. |
| MSST's name | The current official conference title is “International Conference on Massive Storage Systems and Technology.” The biography uses that title rather than copying the CV's older variant. |
| WMCSA founding general chair in 1994; FAST founder and inaugural program chair | CV and [USENIX's inaugural FAST archive](https://www.usenix.org/legacy/events/fast02/). The CV lists FAST's founding program-chair work under 2001; the actual conference was January 2002. The public biography retains the meeting date. |

The CV-year transcription is not a separate verification of every historical
meeting date. For example, its 1992 MASCOTS entry is retained as a CV entry;
the biography does not use that row to assign the conference's founding year.
The original CV has not been edited.
