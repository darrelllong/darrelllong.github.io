Abstract

This solutions manual accompanies Theory of Finite Automata with an Introduction to Formal Languages by John L. Carroll and Darrell D. E. Long. It collects worked solutions chapter by chapter and is maintained as a living edition alongside the restored LaTeX source of the book.
